About this Project

Abstract

Simply walking to school shouldn't be a safety hazard, but unfortunately New Mexico has the highest rate of pedestrian fatalities in the U.S. per-capita - approximately 4.40 to 4.54 deaths per 100,000 population, most occurring in Albuquerque.

This web application maps the highest- and lowest-risk areas around Albuquerque Public Schools, overlaying crash records with school locations to help identify where streets need improvement. The goal is to help city planners prioritize infrastructure upgrades and help parents find the safest routes for their children walking to school.
